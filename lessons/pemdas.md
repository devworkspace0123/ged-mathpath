# GED Math Lesson: Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

## Learning Goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the order of operations.
* Evaluate expressions with parentheses.
* Evaluate expressions with exponents.
* Correctly handle multiplication and division.
* Correctly handle addition and subtraction.
* Solve multi-step GED-style expressions.

---

# 1. What Is the Order of Operations?

The **order of operations** is a set of rules that tells us which operations to perform first.

Without these rules, different people could get different answers to the same problem.

The common memory tool is:

# PEMDAS

$$
\boxed{\text{P}\rightarrow\text{E}\rightarrow\text{MD}\rightarrow\text{AS}}
$$

It stands for:

* **P** — Parentheses
* **E** — Exponents
* **M/D** — Multiplication and Division
* **A/S** — Addition and Subtraction

---

# 2. The Most Important PEMDAS Rule

Multiplication does **not** always come before division.

Addition does **not** always come before subtraction.

Instead:

### Multiplication and Division

Work from left to right.

### Addition and Subtraction

Work from left to right.

Think of PEMDAS as groups:

$$
\boxed{P\rightarrow E\rightarrow MD\rightarrow AS}
$$

---

# 3. Parentheses

Solve operations inside parentheses first.

### Example

Evaluate:

$$
8+(6-2)
$$

First:

$$
6-2=4
$$

Now:

$$
8+4=12
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{12}
$$

---

# 4. Exponents

After parentheses, evaluate exponents.

### Example

Evaluate:

$$
5+3^2
$$

First:

$$
3^2=9
$$

Now:

$$
5+9=14
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{14}
$$

---

# 5. Multiplication and Division

Multiplication and division have equal priority.

Work from left to right.

---

## Example 1

Evaluate:

$$
18\div3\times2
$$

Start from the left:

$$
18\div3=6
$$

Then:

$$
6\times2=12
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{12}
$$

---

## Example 2

Evaluate:

$$
5\times8\div4
$$

Start from the left:

$$
5\times8=40
$$

Then:

$$
40\div4=10
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{10}
$$

---

# 6. Addition and Subtraction

Addition and subtraction also have equal priority.

Work from left to right.

### Example

Evaluate:

$$
20-8+5
$$

Start from the left:

$$
20-8=12
$$

Then:

$$
12+5=17
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{17}
$$

---

# 7. Solving a Multi-Step Expression

Evaluate:

$$
6+2(5-2)^2
$$

---

## Step 1: Parentheses

$$
5-2=3
$$

Now:

$$
6+2(3)^2
$$

---

## Step 2: Exponents

$$
3^2=9
$$

Now:

$$
6+2(9)
$$

---

## Step 3: Multiplication

$$
2\times9=18
$$

Now:

$$
6+18
$$

---

## Step 4: Addition

$$
24
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{24}
$$

---

# 8. Another Multi-Step Example

Evaluate:

$$
24\div(2\times3)+4^2
$$

### Step 1: Parentheses

$$
2\times3=6
$$

Now:

$$
24\div6+4^2
$$

### Step 2: Exponents

$$
4^2=16
$$

Now:

$$
24\div6+16
$$

### Step 3: Division

$$
24\div6=4
$$

Now:

$$
4+16
$$

### Step 4: Addition

$$
20
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{20}
$$

---

# 9. Working with Nested Parentheses

Sometimes parentheses appear inside other parentheses.

Solve the innermost parentheses first.

### Example

Evaluate:

$$
2(3+(4-1))
$$

First:

$$
4-1=3
$$

Now:

$$
2(3+3)
$$

Then:

$$
2(6)=12
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{12}
$$

---

# 10. Common Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Multiplying Before Dividing

Consider:

$$
24\div6\times2
$$

Incorrect approach:

$$
6\times2=12
$$

then:

$$
24\div12=2
$$

This is incorrect.

Correct approach:

$$
24\div6=4
$$

Then:

$$
4\times2=8
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{8}
$$

---

## Mistake 2: Adding Before Subtracting

Consider:

$$
15-4+2
$$

Work from left to right:

$$
15-4=11
$$

$$
11+2=13
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{13}
$$

---

## Mistake 3: Ignoring Exponents

Evaluate:

$$
3+2\times4^2
$$

First:

$$
4^2=16
$$

Then:

$$
2\times16=32
$$

Finally:

$$
3+32=35
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{35}
$$

---

# 11. GED-Style Example

Evaluate:

$$
(12-4)^2\div8+5
$$

### Step 1: Parentheses

$$
12-4=8
$$

### Step 2: Exponents

$$
8^2=64
$$

### Step 3: Division

$$
64\div8=8
$$

### Step 4: Addition

$$
8+5=13
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{13}
$$

---

# 12. Order of Operations Reference

| Step | Operation                                  |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Parentheses and grouping symbols           |
| 2    | Exponents                                  |
| 3    | Multiplication and Division, left to right |
| 4    | Addition and Subtraction, left to right    |

---

# GED Tips

* Parentheses come first.
* Exponents come before multiplication and division.
* Multiplication and division are equal in priority.
* Addition and subtraction are equal in priority.
* Work from left to right when operations have equal priority.
* Show your steps when solving a long expression.
* Do not blindly multiply before dividing.

---

# Memory Trick

## Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally

* **P** → Parentheses
* **E** → Exponents
* **MD** → Multiplication and Division
* **AS** → Addition and Subtraction

The most important idea is:

> **PEMDAS tells you the order of the groups. Within the same group, work from left to right.**
